Prototype — sidebar pins

Hover a project/session to pin. Right-click = menu. Cmd/Ctrl+Up jumps to first pinned. ↑↓ Enter Esc in popup.

Rules: Pinned top by pin-time newest first, uncapped. Projects rest by activity. Per project: pinned fixed max 3 by recency, unpinned collapsed 4 rows / expanded 6-row scroll, collapsed default persisted. Active bumps to row 1 unpinned. 4th pin replaces least-recent with toast. Pins in localStorage.
